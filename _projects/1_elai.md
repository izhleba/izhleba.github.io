As co-founder and Chief R&D Officer of [Elai.io](https://elai.io/), I led the research and development of AI video avatar technology: users type a script and get a presenter video, with no cameras, studios or actors required.

**My role**

- Led the R&D team and set the technical direction for the avatar and text-to-video pipeline.
- Worked with product, design and go-to-market teams to turn research prototypes into features customers use.
- Drove company growth through advanced technology solutions.

**Focus areas:** generative AI for video, AI avatars, text-to-video, e-learning content creation.
